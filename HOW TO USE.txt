PHOTO HANDOUT - How to use
==========================

Everyday use (2 steps):

1) DOWNLOAD photos into the pool
   - Double-click "Download Photos".
   - Type the brand name and how many photos, press Enter.
   - Wait for it to finish, then close the window.

2) HAND OUT photos to freelancers
   - Double-click "Start Photo Handout".
   - A browser tab opens by itself. (Keep the little black window open -
     that is the program running. Closing it stops the tool.)
   - Pick the brand, type each freelancer's name and how many photos.
   - Click "Preview" to check, or "Create & Upload" to send.
   - The page shows a Google Drive link for each freelancer as it finishes.
     Copy the link and send it to that freelancer.
   - Click "View handout history" any time to see who got what, when,
     with their Drive link.

Good to know:
 - A "photo" = the image plus its PSD, counted as 1.
 - Once photos are handed out they are removed from this computer
   automatically (they live in Google Drive) - so nothing piles up here.
 - The same photo is never given out twice, and downloads never re-fetch
   photos that were already handed out.
 - Uploads to Google Drive can take a while for big batches - that's your
   internet's upload speed, not the tool. You can leave it running; links
   appear on the page as each freelancer's upload finishes.

First time on a NEW computer (one-time setup):
 - Install Python from python.org (tick "Add python.exe to PATH").
 - Double-click "Setup (run once)".
 - Make sure these are in this folder / set up: the AWS credentials,
   client_secret.json and token.json (Google Drive sign-in).
 - After that, just use the two steps above.
